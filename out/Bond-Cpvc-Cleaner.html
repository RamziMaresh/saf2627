    Safra Bond (CPVC Cleaner)

    High-performance pipe cleaner specially formulated to prepare pipe surfaces before bonding. It effectively removes dirt, grease, dust, and surface residues, ensuring optimal adhesion when used prior to cement adhesive application.

    • Efficiently cleans and degreases pipe surfaces.
    • Enhances the bonding strength of cement. adhesive.
    • Easy to apply.
    • Suitable for professional and industrial use.
